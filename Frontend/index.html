Physics made Interactive!

This is a physics website. In order to experience the more intricate parts of physics and see them visually in order to understand them, each module contains a simulation that's interactive, completely free to use, and ad-free. It also has analogies and explanations for those who are more interested in the topics, which will help people to understand and get interested in physics (because physics is more than simply a block sliding down a ramp or a ball falling from a mountain). It's a lot more, such as quantum tunneling or electromagnetic induction, which are two of the modules here. The site is also open source, so we will eventually take on new topics as modules. Stay tuned in order to see them.
